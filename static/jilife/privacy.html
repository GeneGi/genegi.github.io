Privacy Policy

Last updated · November 2025

JiLife is a local-first app. We — the developer — collect no data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. This page explains the few places your data can go, all of which you control.

The short version

Everything JiLife records is stored as plain-text files on your own device. We have no servers and receive nothing. The app works fully offline.

Where your data lives

Your timeline, places, people, and entries are written as human-readable org files in a folder — your "vault" — on your device. You can open, read, and edit them with any text editor at any time. They are never uploaded to us.

The only times data leaves your device

Three things can transmit data, all under your control:

  • iCloud sync. If you enable it, your vault syncs through your own iCloud account to your other Apple devices. This is Apple's standard document sync — the data stays in your iCloud, governed by Apple's privacy policy. We never see it.
  • Place-name lookup. When you arrive somewhere new, the app may make a one-time reverse-geocode request to name the place — a coordinate goes out, a name comes back. Only that single coordinate is sent, only at that moment, and you can disable it entirely; entries simply show coordinates instead of a name.
  • Your own git remote. Optionally, you can point the app at a git repository you control (for example a private GitHub repo) to keep a history of your vault. This is configured by you, uses your own credentials, and syncs only to the remote you choose. It is off by default.

What the app can access, and why

With your permission, JiLife reads the following on your device:

  • Location — to build your timeline of where you went, including in the background if you enable it.
  • Motion & fitness — to tell walking, driving, and still time apart.
  • Health data — to add sleep and workout snapshots to your entries. Read-only; never written back.
  • Calendar — to suggest what a detected visit was, for you to confirm. Never leaves the device.
  • Microphone & speech — only while you dictate an entry, transcribed on-device.
  • Photos & camera — only when you attach a photo to an entry yourself.

Every one of these is processed on your device and stored in your local vault. None of it is sent to us or any third party for any other purpose.

Data we collect

None. JiLife has:

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No crash-reporting SDKs that phone home
  • No advertising or third-party trackers
  • No servers operated by the developer

On Apple's App Store privacy label, JiLife reports "Data Not Collected."

Your data is yours

Because everything lives in files on your device, deleting the app and its vault deletes your data completely — there is nothing on our side to remove. Backups and exports are simply copies of your own files, which you already hold.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated date above. Since we collect nothing, changes would only ever describe new optional features you choose to use.

Questions? Reach us through GitHub Issues — that's the fastest way to get a real answer about privacy or anything else.
