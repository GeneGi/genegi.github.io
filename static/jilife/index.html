How it works

Automatic timeline

JiLife watches motion and location to build your day as it happens — where you stopped, how you got there, how long you stayed. No check-ins, no manual logging unless you want to.

Correct it like a journal

The guess is always a question, never a silent write. Rename a place, fix a time, add what it missed — the timeline stays yours to edit.

Insights, on-device

Patterns surface from your own history: recurring places, sleep, how your weeks actually look. Analysis happens on your phone, not our servers — we don't have any.

Plain files, yours forever

Everything lives as human-readable org files you can open in any editor. No account, no lock-in, no export step — it was never anywhere else.

Local-first

We collect nothing. JiLife has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. The app works entirely on your device.

The only times data leaves your phone are ones you choose: your own iCloud for sync between your devices, a one-time lookup to name a place you visited, and an optional git remote you configure yourself. Read the privacy policy.
