Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Everything JiLife records lives as plain-text org files in a folder — your vault — on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us. If you enable iCloud sync, the files travel through your own iCloud account to your other Apple devices.

Do you collect any data about me?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no developer servers — the app works fully offline. The privacy policy lists the few places data can leave your device, all of which you control.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app and its vault deletes your data completely — there is nothing on our side to remove, because nothing was ever sent to us.

How do I request a feature?

Email jilifeapp@gmail.com with what you'd like JiLife to do. Feature requests go straight onto the roadmap pile — the more specific the scenario, the better.
